Bottle Stand

The ring on your fishing pole is hooked on the bottle’s neck. Hold your finger down and slide it to move the pole and slowly pull the bottle up straight.

Go easy! The string turns red when you pull too hard, and if you yank it the ring slips off.

When the bottle is standing straight, lift your finger to let go. If it stays standing, you win the round. If it tips over, you lose one of 3 tries.

Beat the clock. Each round the clock is shorter and the bottle is wobblier. Every 5th round is a 🟡 gold bottle that pays 🪙 +1.
