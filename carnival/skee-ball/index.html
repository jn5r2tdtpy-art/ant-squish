Skee-Ball

Slide your finger up the lane to roll the ball. Start your swipe where you want the ball across the lane, angle it to steer, and swipe faster to roll it farther up the board.

The ball hops the bump and rolls down into a hole: three 20s along the bottom, two 30s, the 40 in the middle and two 50s up high. Land on a hole or just above it to drop in; anything else rolls into the 10 slot. Too soft and it rolls back for nothing.

You get 9 balls a round. Reach the goal score to beat the round, or lose one of 3 tries. Each round the goal goes up, and later the board starts sliding side to side.

The tiny 100 pockets in the top corners pay 🪙 +1 each.
