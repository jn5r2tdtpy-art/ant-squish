Knock Over
the Cans

Knock all the cans off the table with 2 balls. Every 10 rounds the pyramid grows by a row, and you get 1 more ball.

Tap once to lock your aim as the marker slides across the table. Tap again to lock how high you throw as the marker slides up and down beside the cans.

Hit the bottom row and the cans above come down too. Too low hits the table, too high flies over.

Knock down the gold can to win 🪙 +1. Each round the markers get faster and the cans move farther back.

Leave cans standing and you lose one of 3 tries.
