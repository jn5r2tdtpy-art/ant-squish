Knock Down
Gabby Abby

Fire the air cannon into Abby’s mouth and knock out all 6 teeth before the clock runs out. Each tooth is 10.

From round 2 her teeth grow back if you’re too slow, and faster every round.

From round 3 some teeth wear braces: the first hit knocks the braces off, the second knocks the tooth out for 20.

When the light on top flashes, the gold tooth is worth double.

Hurry: her mouth slowly closes as the clock runs down.
