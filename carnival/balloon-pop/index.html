Pop Sonny’s Ego

“Sorry, Sonny. Didn’t mean to bust your balloon!”

Every balloon on the board has Sonny’s face on it, and he is very proud of them. Throw darts and bust Sonny’s balloons. You get 5 darts a round. Pop enough to beat the round.

Tap once to lock where across the board as the marker slides side to side. Tap again to lock how high you throw as the power meter beside the board slides up and down.

Each round the markers get faster and the balloons get smaller, and from round 3 some balloons are missing.

Pop the shiny gold balloon to win 🪙 +1. Miss the goal and you lose one of 3 tries.
