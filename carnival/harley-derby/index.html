Sonny’s
Harley Derby

Race your Harley against Sonny, Eddie and Abby! Roll the ball into a hole to move your bike ahead: roll it up the lane to the triangle of holes at the end. The 🟡 front holes move you 1, the 🔴 middle row 2 and the 🔵 back row 3. Miss and you go nowhere.

Slide your finger up the lane to roll the ball. Start your swipe where you want the ball across the lane, angle it to steer, and swipe faster to roll it farther.

Beat all three of them to the checkered flag to win the race. Each race they ride faster. Lose a race and you lose one of 3 tries.

Each race you win pays 🪙 +1.

Your rider wears a helmet. Put your own head on your bike!
