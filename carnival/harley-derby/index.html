Sonny’s
Harley Derby

Sonny’s racing his Harley against 3 other riders. Roll the ball into a hole to move him ahead: roll it up the lane to the triangle of holes at the end. The 🟡 front holes move him 1, the 🔴 middle row 2 and the 🔵 back row 3. Miss and he goes nowhere.

Tap once to lock where across the ramp as the arrow slides side to side. Tap again to lock how hard you roll as the power meter beside the ramp slides up and down.

Beat the other riders to the checkered flag to win the race. Each race they ride faster. Lose a race and you lose one of 3 tries.

Each race Sonny wins pays 🪙 +1.
