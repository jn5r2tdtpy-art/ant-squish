Hoop Shot

Swipe up to shoot. Start your swipe where you want the ball across, angle it to steer, and swipe faster to shoot farther. Too soft hits the front of the rim; a little long can bank off the backboard.

You have 30 seconds. Shoot as fast as you can: each basket is 2 points, and 3 in the last 10 seconds of 🔥 bonus time.

Score the goal to beat the round, or lose one of 3 tries. The goal goes up each round, and from round 3 the hoop slides side to side.

Make the 🟡 gold ball (one each round) to win 🪙 +1.
