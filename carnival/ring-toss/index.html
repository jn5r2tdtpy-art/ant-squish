Eddie’s Wedding
Ring Toss

Eddie’s been married four times, so he’s got plenty of wedding rings to toss. Land one over a peg for a RINGER!

Tap once to lock your aim as the marker slides across the board. Tap again to lock how far you throw as the marker slides up and down the rows.

You get 5 rings a round. Get enough ringers to beat the round. The back rows are worth more.

Ring the 💎 diamond peg to win 🪙 +1. Miss the goal and you lose one of 3 tries.
