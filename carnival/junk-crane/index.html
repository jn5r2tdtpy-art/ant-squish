The Junk Crane

Win an egg. Open it. Regret it.

Drag your finger along the slider to move the claw over an egg, then tap the big red DROP button. The claw drops, grabs and carries the egg to the prize chute.

Line the claw up right over the middle of an egg: the closer you are, the better it holds. Watch out, the claw swings a little on its cable when you drop it, more if it’s still moving, so let it stop first. Sometimes it slips anyway. It’s a claw machine.

You get 5 tries. Every egg you win, you open, and every prize inside is junk: Eddie’s debt, Sonny’s spare Harley keys, the penny Abby found on the floor…

The shiny 🟡 gold egg has a real coin inside: 🪙 +1.
